SafeWatch Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026. Questions? Email ozanbakis@gmail.com. Türkçe

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. SafeWatch has no accounts, no advertising of its own and no tracking or analytics.

What stays on your device

Your watch history, hidden channels and words, limits, parental control settings and the parental PIN are stored only on your device. Watch history is deleted automatically after 30 days. Deleting the app removes this data.

What is sent to our server

To load videos, the app sends our server your country code, the words you search for, and the IDs of the channels you watch most (so it can suggest their new videos). This information is not linked to you, and it is not logged or stored, apart from short-lived caching of search results so the same search isn't repeated. Our server runs on Cloudflare, which may process your IP address to deliver the request.

YouTube

Videos are provided by YouTube through YouTube API Services and play in YouTube's own player. When a video plays, YouTube may collect data as described in the Google Privacy Policy. By using SafeWatch you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Children

SafeWatch does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Parental controls work entirely on the device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, they are created on your device. No push service or device token is used.

Your rights

Because we don't hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can clear your history in the app at any time. For any request under the GDPR, KVKK or similar laws, email us.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
