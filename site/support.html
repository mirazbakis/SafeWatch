FAQ

How do I turn on parental controls?

Open Settings in the app and tap "Turn on parental controls", then set a 4-digit PIN. Limits, bedtime, breaks, content rules and the activity report are then available behind that PIN.

I forgot my parental PIN. What do I do?

For safety, the PIN can't be reset from inside the app. Delete and reinstall SafeWatch, or email us for help.

Do I need an account?

No. SafeWatch has no accounts. Your history and settings stay on your device.

Why can't I find a video?

SafeWatch filters out clickbait and unsuitable videos, and parental controls may hide some categories or channels. Some videos also can't be played outside YouTube or in your country.

Something isn't working. What should I include in my email?

Your device model, iOS version, and a short description or screenshot of the problem.
