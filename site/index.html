Get SafeWatch in three steps.

Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.

  1. 1

    Add the DNS profile

    Needed for the installs on this page.

    • Tap Get Profile, then Allow.
    • Settings › Profile Downloaded › Install.
    • General › VPN & Device Management › DNS › SafeWatch.

  2. 2

Help

Questions

Is SafeWatch free?

Yes. There's no account and nothing to sign up for.

Tapping Install does nothing.

Open this page in Safari, tick the Terms box and add the DNS profile first. If a signed install still won't start, its certificate may be revoked. Pick another one.

It installed but won't open.

Trust it in Settings › General › VPN & Device Management. If it still closes right away, that install's certificate was revoked. Delete it and try another signed install.

Are there limits if I'm not a parent?

No time limits unless a parent turns on parental controls. Shorts come in collections of 20; reopen the app for a new one.

What data does SafeWatch collect?

None about you. History, settings and the PIN stay on your phone. To load videos the app only sends your country, your searches and the channels you watch most, none of it linked to you.
