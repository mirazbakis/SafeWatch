Questions

When can I get SafeWatch?

SafeWatch is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

Is SafeWatch free?

Yes. There's no account and nothing to sign up for.

Are there limits if I'm not a parent?

No time limits unless a parent turns on parental controls. Shorts come in collections of 20; reopen the app for a new one.

What data does SafeWatch collect?

None about you. History, settings and the PIN stay on your phone. To load videos the app only sends your country, your searches and the channels you watch most, none of it linked to you. See the Privacy Policy.

Where do the videos come from?

Videos are public YouTube videos, shown through YouTube API Services in YouTube's own player. SafeWatch is not made, endorsed or sponsored by YouTube or Google.
